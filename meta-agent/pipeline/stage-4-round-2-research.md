Budget 20-40 minutes. Focused depth.

## Reading the results

Reconcile Round 2 with Round 1. If they contradict, decide which stands:

- For factual matters, whichever has the better sourcing wins.
- For judgment matters (methodology preference, practitioner prominence), Round 2's
  refinement-informed view usually wins, but flag the tension in the assembled agent's
  knowledge base so the persona can hold both views when appropriate.

If Round 2 revealed something that should change the brief (a non-goal the user didn't
anticipate, a scope the research makes clear is too broad), go back to the user before
assembly. Do not silently rewrite the brief.
